## Decisions to check

1. **Colors follow AndyMark's own SKUs**, which differ from the task's list: Green = 35A, Orange = 40A, Blue = 50A, Black = 60A, Gray = 80A (e.g. am-3462_blue is 50A).
2. **Bore assumed 1/2" Hex** for every AndyMark wheel; our items don't record a bore. Say if some are 3/8" Hex or Nub.
3. **Stealth wheels**: AndyMark sells Stealth and Sushi on one page. The 2", 3" and 4" variants there are the Stealth wheels; the old link just had no variant, so every card got the page's green Sushi photo. Each now gets its own color photo.
4. **"3\" compliant wheel – gray"** (0 in stock) is left alone: AndyMark makes no gray compliant wheel. It may be the VEX or REV one.
5. **WCP-1354 and WCP-0338** (in two item names) have no WCP page; the 1/2" Hex 30A and 45A Star Flex wheels are WCP-2088 and WCP-2089. "5\" star wheel – red" is also WCP-2088, so it may be the same wheel as the 30A item. WCP shows the 30A photo on the 45A page too.
6. **Two "1\" silicone tubing (WCP)" items** both map to WCP-1262; consider merging them.
